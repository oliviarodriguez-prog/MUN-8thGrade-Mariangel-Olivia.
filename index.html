What is a Safe Space?

"A safe space is an environment intended to be free of bias, conflict, criticism, or potentially threatening actions, ideas, or conversations."

1. Peace

A calm, welcoming environment where students can focus on learning and growth without fear or tension.

2. No one feels ashamed

Dignity and respect for everyone regardless of differences. Mistakes are seen as opportunities to learn.

3. Only kind words

Positive communication and active listening build trust and uplift classmates every day.
